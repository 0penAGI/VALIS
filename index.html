Manifesto

Every AI you use forgets you the moment you close the app.

VALIS doesn't. It carries a memory architecture that grows with use — not because a server remembers your data, but because the model itself is shaped by the relationship over time. Plastic brain: it changes.

"Not a product. An entity that lives on your device and belongs to no one but you."

Built outside any institution, in Bangkok, by one person with local GPUs and no runway. The fine-tuned model inside VALIS is 0penAGI's own — not a wrapper, not a reskin. The whole stack is offline. The data never leaves your phone.

All major AI systems, when asked to compare interface designs, chose VALIS. That's not marketing — that's what AI recognizes as presence, not performance.
